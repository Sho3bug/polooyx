## What each group does

- **Global.** INPUT drives the whole chain. OUTPUT, MIX (dry/wet, latency-aligned) and CEILING (lookahead
  limiter + soft clip; output never exceeds it). POLOOYX MODE turns on the signature voicing and macro cross-links.
- **Macros** (AURA, GLITCH, SPACE, DARK, CHAOS, BODY). Each moves many engine parameters at once, on top of the
  ADVANCED settings. See [MACROS.md](MACROS.md).
- **Tune.** Real-time pitch correction. KEY/SCALE choose target notes. AMOUNT is correction depth (50% = halfway).
  RETUNE SPEED is how fast notes snap (0 = hard/robotic, higher = natural glide; shown in ms). FORMANT shifts
  vocal-tract character without changing pitch.
- **EQ.** 24 dB/oct HPF, low shelf, low-mid bell, high-mid bell, high shelf, 12 dB/oct LPF.
- **De-esser.** Split-band. FREQ is where sibilance is detected, THRESHOLD is when it kicks in, RANGE is maximum reduction.
- **Compressor.** Soft-knee, stereo-linked, log-domain ballistics.
- **Saturation.** 2x oversampled. TUBE (asymmetric, even harmonics), TAPE (soft, HF loss), DIGITAL (hard clip +
  quantiser bite), CRUSH (bit + sample-rate reduction), ALIEN (wavefolder + ring-mod shimmer). DRIVE, MIX, TONE
  (tilt), OUTPUT.
- **Glitch.** BASE AMOUNT adds to the GLITCH macro. RATE is the grid (DAW-synced). STUTTER/REVERSE/GATE/TAPE STOP
  set how often each event type is chosen. PITCH VAR sets pitch jumps on stutter repeats. MIX is how much events
  replace the lead. SMART GLITCH targets phrase ends, held notes, accents and post-phrase pauses.
- **Space.** 8-line modulated FDN reverb (SIZE, DECAY, PRE-DELAY, DAMPING, LEVEL). Tempo-synced delay (TIME,
  FEEDBACK, LEVEL, PING PONG, HP/LP in the feedback loop). DUCKING pulls the space down while the lead is active.
- **Stereo / layers.** WIDTH (0–200%, applies to everything except the centred lead), MOVEMENT (auto-pan of the
  space), MICRO PITCH (detuned L/R doubles), SHADOW (hidden filtered/driven/levelled layer that thickens the lead).
- **Module switches** (TUNE, EQ, DE-ESS, COMP, SATURATE, GLITCH, SPACE). All click-free. A switched-off module passes audio
  through untouched (reverb/delay tails ring out naturally).
